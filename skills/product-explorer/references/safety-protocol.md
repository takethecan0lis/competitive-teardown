# Safety protocol (default; the user may tighten or loosen it per product)

The tester's account and phone are real. Exploration must leave them exactly as found.

## The agent never
- Saves, submits, completes, checks off, logs, posts, sends, invites, or rates anything.
- Buys, starts a trial, upgrades, gifts, or opens a purchase confirmation on purpose.
- Deletes, archives, resets, or exports data.
- Changes settings, permissions, notifications, or privacy options.
- Signs in, types passwords, enters codes, or accepts terms.
- Records personal content: documents, records, contacts, messages, notifications, lock-screen text. Structure only.
- Captures other people: names, photos, messages or profiles on social, team or shared screens. Describe the structure instead. Screenshots and recordings stay in evidence/sessions and are never packaged.

## The agent may
- Navigate, scroll, open menus, info icons, and disclosures.
- Open create and edit forms to record their fields, then cancel without saving (create-and-cancel).
- Read help, settings, and paywall screens without acting on them.

## Stay inside the target app
Stay inside the target app. Never open other apps, notifications, Control Center, Messages, Wallet or Settings. If a purchase, Apple Pay, OS permission or sign-in sheet appears, stop and hand off. Loosening the protocol never covers purchases, sends, sign-ins or other people. Screenshots stay in the session folder, are never packaged, and are retaken if they show personal content.

## The user does (through guided handoff)
- Anything above in "never", when it is needed for research, after the agent explains what and why and the user agrees to that specific action.

## Incident handling
If any action changes state by accident: stop, describe exactly what changed, reverse it right away (Discard, Undo, back out, revert the field), verify the reversal on screen, and log it in the session notes under "Protocol incidents". Tell the user in the same turn.

## Adjusting the protocol
At the start of a teardown, ask whether the account holds real data. For throwaway test accounts the user may allow the agent to save test objects; record that decision in the charter. For accounts with real data, keep the default and add: no opening of export or share sheets.
