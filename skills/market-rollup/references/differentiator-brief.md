# One-page differentiator brief

Audience: the enterprise product manager deciding whether to invest.

1. **The market in two sentences:** who the players are and what they all do.
2. **The gap:** the unmet need, in the user's words, with the evidence (teardown IDs, review themes).
3. **Our differentiator:** one sentence. Then three bullets on why it is defensible.
4. **Who it serves first:** the persona, and why they will switch or start.
5. **Why now:** the timing or market signal.
6. **Riskiest assumption and the test:** what must be true, how we will know within weeks, at what cost.
7. **Expected return:** the value hypothesis in business hypothesis form, and the first measurable signal.
8. **Ask:** the decision needed and by when.

Keep to one page. Link to the comparison sheet and market view for detail.
