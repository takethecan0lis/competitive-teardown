# Comparison sheet

## Tab 1: Feature matrix
`capability, category, job_to_be_done, <competitor_1>_score, <competitor_1>_evidence, ..., best_in_market, table_stakes(Y/N), notes`
Scores: 0 absent, 1 weak, 2 basic, 3 solid, 4 strong, 5 best in market. Evidence: the CAP ID and tag from that teardown.

## Tab 4: Privacy and safety
`competitor, data_location, encryption, third_party_sharing, contacts_or_permissions, deletion_path, abuse_reporting, red_flags, source`

## Tab 5: Scale and company
`competitor, installs_or_users, ratings, release_cadence, team_size, profitability_or_funding, confidence, source`

## Tab 6: Scores summary
Weighted totals by job to be done, with the weights stated and editable.
