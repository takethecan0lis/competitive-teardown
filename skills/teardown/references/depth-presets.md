# Depth presets

All presets write to the same schema, so a teardown can be upgraded later without starting over.

## Quick scan (about 1 hour, no device needed)
- Steps: 0, 1, 3 (from desk evidence), 7.
- Sources: store listings, help center, top review themes, pricing, privacy policy, company site and jobs.
- Outputs: charter, capability inventory (confidence capped at Medium), tone snapshot (10 to 20 terms), pricing and packaging, open questions, summary doc.
- Use for: triaging a long list of competitors down to the few that matter.

## Standard (default; half a day plus one guided session)
- Steps: 0 to 7.
- Adds: one guided walkthrough with full MAP coverage, create-and-cancel on every object type, edit forms opened on one existing item per type, journeys, relationship model, tone and lexicon study, feedback design, gap analysis, WSJF opportunities, risks, team question bank, verifier pass.
- Use for: any competitor that will appear in a market roll-up.

## Full teardown (several sessions over days)
- Steps: 0 to 7 with every lens.
- Adds: the tester's own data export (structure only), real-moment sessions at real times (scheduled reminders, events, notifications), screen recordings as evidence, the company's own public docs and change-rationale FAQs, architecture and data model with diagrams, lexicon and taxonomy, signature-mechanic deep dive with research backing, company history and strategy, session plan around trial or paywall deadlines.
- Use for: the two or three rivals whose design would change what you build.

## Upgrading
To upgrade quick to standard or standard to full, re-read the charter, keep all IDs, add sessions, and raise confidence only where new evidence supports it.
