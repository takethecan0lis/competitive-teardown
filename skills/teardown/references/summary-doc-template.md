# Summary doc template (one per competitor; neutral)

Title: <Product> teardown summary (<depth>, <date>)

1. **In one paragraph:** what the product is, who it serves, how it makes money, and the one thing it does best.
2. **Headline findings (5 to 7):** each one sentence with its evidence tag and a pointer to the KB file.
3. **Core loop:** a short description or diagram of how a user gets value day to day.
4. **Strengths and weaknesses:** two short lists, each item tagged.
5. **Tone and stance:** how the product speaks to its users and where the voice breaks.
6. **Monetization:** free versus paid, prices, complaints.
7. **Risks and red flags:** privacy, safety, stability, dependence on a platform.
8. **Open questions that need a human:** the top 3 to 5 with owners.
9. **Coverage and confidence:** depth run, MAP coverage (for example 142 of 156 screens), sessions held, share of claims by tag.
10. **Where to read more:** reading order into the KB.

Keep it to two pages. No comparison to the user's own product here.
