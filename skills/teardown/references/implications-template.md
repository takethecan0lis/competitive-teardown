# Implications for us (written only after research is confirmed complete)

Location: `implications/<product>-implications.md`, outside the evidence folder. Read the user's persona, empathy map, and product vision first. Never edit evidence files from this step.

1. **What this product proves:** demand, behaviors, or mechanics the market has validated (cite KB IDs).
2. **What to learn, not copy:** principles worth adopting, restated in our own words.
3. **Where it leaves room:** unmet needs, underserved segments, complaints we could resolve (cite GAP, THM, review themes).
4. **Where it threatens us:** strengths we cannot match quickly; switching costs it holds.
5. **Our persona versus its users:** fit and misfit, using the persona supplied by the user.
6. **Candidate hypotheses for us:** written as business hypothesis statements (We believe that... for... will result in... We will know we are right when...). Hand these to the product manager agent.
7. **Things we must not do:** legal and trademark boundaries, ethical lines this product crossed.
